# Council protocol

## Seat packet
One decision/target, business facts, objective, constraints, options (including a smaller reversible option), dated evidence ledger, selected seat dossier, response schema, and round-specific context. Keep facts and assumptions separate. We do not attempt to reconstruct a person's private mind. The dossier is a professional lens and authored character.

## Opening
Give the selected seat only its packet, not other seats' conclusions. Produce one `turn`: `id`, `seat`, `round: opening`, `text`, `challenge`, `stance`, `evidenceIds`, `fictional: true`.

Prompt: Identify the most important constraint through your professional lens. State one useful finding and one concrete action. Cite relevant ledger IDs. Explain why the action matters for this business; test the user's preferred answer. In `challenge`, state what you need to know or what observation would change your view. Unknown facts yield `unknown`, not confident speculation. Prefer 100–180 words; a sharp line is welcome if it attacks a tactic rather than a person.

## Challenge
Provide the first round and exact evidence. Ask each seat to address the strongest competing argument by name. Identify whether disagreement is factual, a time horizon, business priority or risk tolerance. Concede any point better supported by evidence. Name one discriminating test and a stop condition. Do not turn independent seats into a scripted chorus. Convergence is allowed when justified; artificial disagreement is not required.

## Final position (full council)
Read the chair's candidate resolution. State support/conditional/challenge/unknown, the condition that matters and any remaining material objection. No new unsupported facts. One short response per seat. Never rewrite a seat's prior position silently.

## Chair
Do not average unsupported ordinal scores into a scientific-looking SEO score. Assess severity, evidence, confidence, business relevance, reversibility and implementation effort separately. A blocking defect with high-quality evidence can dominate several speculative benefits. An unknown objective or missing key baseline can justify `hold` or a measurement `test`. Preserve dissent even if a decision is unanimous on immediate actions.

Suggested priority: P0 for a verified critical blocker or imminent harm; P1 for a material near-term improvement/test; P2 for contingent or lower-impact work. These are planning labels, not predicted traffic lifts.

## Personality without impersonation
Write fictional dialogue in a distinct editorial voice derived from the dossier, not alleged real quotes. No private life speculation, fabricated success stories, imitation of accents, or abusive attacks. Unsupported statement from an expert still requires scrutiny. A dated practitioner claim can be useful and wrong at the same time.

## Freshness checks
Use current official Google Search Central/Business Profile documentation for indexing, schema eligibility, reviews, spam and AI search behavior. Use official Bing documentation for Bing reports and official WordPress/plugin docs for compatibility. A creator's tool walkthrough or forecast is not proof of current product availability. Research samples include incomplete ASR and editorial summaries; exact quotation requires checking the original.
