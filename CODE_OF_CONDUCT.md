# Community conduct

Challenge claims, methods and evidence. Treat contributors and featured creators with respect.

- Keep criticism specific and useful. Do not harass, threaten, impersonate or target people over personal characteristics.
- Welcome questions, corrections and differences in experience. Explain an objection and what evidence would resolve it.
- Protect private client information. Remove credentials, analytics identifiers and personal details from examples and bug reports.
- Clearly distinguish authored character dialogue from a real person's words. Do not imply endorsement or affiliation.
- Use English for public project documentation and issue discussions so the maintainer can keep a shared record. Users may run councils in any language. A translation or concise English summary is welcome.

The maintainer may edit or remove abusive or privacy-violating content and restrict repeated disruption. Use GitHub's private reporting tools for abuse; do not publish sensitive evidence in an issue. For technical vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). For attribution or portrayal corrections, follow [docs/ATTRIBUTION.md](docs/ATTRIBUTION.md).
